A FIELD GUIDE FOR SMALL HEROES

Keep the pear.
Lose the chickens.

Build your gnome guardians on the stone clearings beside the trail. They attack automatically when the flock comes within range.

  1. Recruit. Select a guardian, then click a numbered clearing.
  2. Defend. Enemies have 2.7× their original health (10% less than the previous balance). Send a wave. Defeated birds earn gold; escaped birds damage the shrine.
  3. Adapt. During any wave in Level 1, a placed KnowME unlocks one five-second board freeze per game. Clear rewards rise each wave, with an extra 500 gold after Level 1 wave 5 and 1,000 after wave 10. Venom cocks spit acid at nearby towers. Guardians have 100 health, plus 35 per upgrade. Destroyed guardians are lost with no refund: buy a replacement on their clearing.
  4. Outlast. Survive 15 forest waves, 18 ashen waves, and 20 final waves. Lady Never Scared unlocks in Level 2; Phil Heal unlocks in Level 3. Reno Mo costs 1,500 gold and destroys half the birds currently on-screen (rounded up), with a 45-combat-second cooldown.
